Content that gets seen, ads that bring in leads, and reporting that shows what it all cost. Made in South Wales, for businesses across Wales and the UK.

DRVN Social is a social media marketing agency based in Ebbw Vale, working with businesses across South Wales and the rest of the UK. We're a film crew and a growth team under one roof. We make the content, run the ads, and report on what it actually brought in.

What we do

  • Strategy and content planning. Audience research, platform choice and a plan of attack built around your goals.
  • Short-form and social content. Reels, TikToks and Shorts, shot by our own crew and made for the feed.
  • Paid social. Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok and YouTube ads, run against leads and cost per lead, not likes.
  • YouTube series and podcasts. Long-form content that builds trust and keeps people watching.
  • Community management. Replies, comments and DMs handled, so enquiries don't go cold.
  • Reporting. Clear monthly reports on views, leads and what each one cost.
